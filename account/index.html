Account & data

Effective October 18, 2026

You can delete a Sky Hopper account from inside the game. This page is for the Android app published by hitrocker, package name com.skyhopper.game.

Delete the account in the game

  1. Open Sky Hopper.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  4. Tap TAP AGAIN TO DELETE to confirm.

The same button is available from the sign-in screen. It removes a guest account, an email account, or a Google account. If the game says to sign in again, sign in, then repeat the steps.

What is deleted

  • The sign-in, including a guest account, an email account, or the Google link, and the account identifier.
  • The leaderboard entry: the display name and the best score.

Those cloud records are removed when the deletion finishes. They are not kept for an extra period.

What stays

  • The best score and settings already saved on that phone stay on the phone until Sky Hopper is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

Ask by email

If you cannot open the game, send a request to hitrocker27@gmail.com from the email on the account, or include the leaderboard name. The same cloud account and leaderboard entry listed above will be deleted.
