Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Sky Hopper is made by hitrocker. This policy describes the information the Android app collects and how it is used. The app package name is com.hitrocker.skyhopper.

Information the game stores

The game creates a guest account so a score can be saved. You can also sign in with email or Google. Depending on how you play, the game can store:

  • An account identifier created by Firebase Authentication.
  • Your email address, if you create an email account or sign in with email.
  • The Google account used to sign in, if you choose Google.
  • The name you choose to show on the leaderboard.
  • Your best score.

Sound, music, haptics, dark mode, and your best score are also saved on the phone so the game can open without a network connection. The game does not show ads and does not sell anything inside the app.

How it is used

The information is used to sign you in, keep your best score, and show the leaderboard. It is not sold.

Where it is stored

Account and leaderboard records are stored with Google Firebase. Firebase is operated by Google. Their processing of that data is covered by Google’s terms and privacy policy as well as this one.

How long it is kept

Cloud records stay until you delete the account, as described on the Account & data page. A best score and settings saved only on the phone stay on that phone until you uninstall Sky Hopper or clear its storage.

Children

Sky Hopper is not directed at children under 13, and a child under 13 should not create an account.

Changes
